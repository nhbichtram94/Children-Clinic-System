# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Overview

The Children's Clinic Management System was developed to digitize clinic operations including patient management, appointment scheduling, medical records, and billing.

## Business Objectives

- Reduce paperwork
- Improve patient management
- Manage appointments efficiently
- Support doctors and receptionists
- Generate reports automatically

## Stakeholders

- Administrator
- Doctor
- Receptionist
- Guardian

## Business Scope

The system supports:

- Patient management
- Appointment booking
- Medical records
- Billing
- User management
- Reports
